About Me

Freelance Graphic Designer

Hi, I'm Shahzaib Sohail, a passionate Freelance Graphic Designer with 5+ years of professional experience in creating visually compelling designs that help businesses, brands, and creators make a lasting impression.

I specialize in Movie Poster Design and YouTube Thumbnail Design, crafting attention-grabbing visuals that increase engagement and strengthen brand identity. My expertise also includes Logo Design, Branding, Business Cards, Flyers, Brochures, Banners, and a wide range of digital and print marketing materials.

With every project, I combine creativity, strategic thinking, and attention to detail to transform ideas into powerful visual content that delivers real results.
